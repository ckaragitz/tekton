# famgen-ci / #530 — a cylinder measures the fixed-chord extrapolation it carries

Fragment of the `famgen-ci` stream (index: `docs/inbox/famgen-ci.md`). Nobody
else appends to this file.

## What the issue asked for, and what was actually possible

`G.cylinder` tessellates its curved rails with hard constants —
`ARC_CHORDS_FIRST_HALF = 7`, `ARC_CHORDS_SECOND_HALF = 6` — mined from ONE
specimen (614's legs, radius 0.125 ft) and applied at every radius with no
radius term. The issue's first DONE asked for a chord count *derived* from
radius.

**That was not done, deliberately.** Deriving a radius law would mean inventing
one: the tree flags the mechanism `[H]` — "why 7 vs 6 for equal spans" is a
hypothesis, and if Revit sized the two halves from a chord tolerance the counts
do not transfer. There is no second-radius specimen in a fresh clone
(`samples/`, `vendor/`, `extracted/` absent), so `reproduce_specimen_cylinders`
cannot even run here. Fitting a curve through one point and shipping it as the
format's law is exactly what this engine refuses to do with a manufacturer's
dimension; it is no better with a tessellation.

So this takes the issue's stated alternative: **measure the extrapolation and
label it**, leaving the constants untouched.

## Why it is not cosmetic

Worth recording, because the first instinct is that edge nodes are display-only
and Revit will regenerate them. `docs/writer/family-geometry.md` §4.4 says
otherwise — the solid's stored extents ARE the node extents:

> The solid's `m_bBox/m_tightbBox` = the extents of the stored edge NODES
> (614's ymin = −0.5 − 0.125·sin(3π/7) = −0.62187, the 7-chord node — **NOT the
> analytic −0.625**) — reproduced exactly by taking our node bbox.

And #515 already burned the "Revit rebuilds it" assumption once: *"The
regeneration rep is a myth … inherited from walls, never verified for a family
form, and false."* A 16 in shade is therefore drawn, and bounded, as a 13-gon.

## What was built

- `geometry.ARC_CHORDS_VERIFIED_RADIUS_FT = 0.125` — the one radius on record,
  named rather than implicit in a comment.
- `geometry.arc_tessellation_facts(radius_ft)` → `chords`, `sagitta_ft` (the
  chord-to-arc deviation of the coarser half — how far the stored nodes sit
  inside the true circle), `specimen_ratio`, `verified` (True only at 0.125 ft).
- `G.cylinder` attaches those facts to the bundle and, away from the verified
  radius, appends a note naming the deviation and #530.
- The family report's form serializer now also carries `radius_ft` and
  `tessellation` — previously it whitelisted box keys only, so a cylinder's
  report said nothing about its own extents.

## Measured

| part | radius_ft | ×specimen | chords | sagitta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| specimen 614 | 0.12500 | 1.0× | 13 | 0.051 in (**verified**) |
| 0.75 in stem | 0.03125 | 0.25× | 13 | 0.013 in |
| 5 in canopy | 0.20833 | 1.7× | 13 | 0.085 in |
| 16 in shade | 0.66667 | 5.3× | 13 | **0.273 in** |

The count is 13 at every radius — that is the finding, now on the record of
every family that contains a cylinder.

## Open, and what would close it

The law itself. Closing #530 properly needs a **donor `.rfa` with cylinders at
a second radius** — owner machine or `$RVT_FAMILY_DONOR`. With two points the
7/6 split either transfers (constants are correct, this note becomes noise and
should be deleted) or it does not (the count is a function, and the derived
form can be written with evidence). Until then a caller sees the extrapolation
and its size instead of a silent number.
