## 1. Partitions carving is incomplete — the "gaps" hold data (A6 / A1 owners)

racbasic `Partitions/15`: the 1,068 carved gzip members cover only
16,528,659 of 18,508,710 raw bytes. The 1,069 gaps (1.98 MB, entropy ~7.996)
are NOT empty framing:

- **164 gaps contain an uncompressed record** beginning with the AString
  `Data generated by Autodesk® Revit®` (34 chars, u32 len prefix), followed
  by ElementIds `a3 03 ff ff ff ff a2 03`, count `e1 00 00 00`, the
  application GUID `bytes_le 00 26 b2 34 d6 3e b3 44 b4 f1 65 96 f4 d5 2b 43`
  (= 34b22600-3ed6-44b3-b4f1-6596f4d52b43, also stamped before every
  `"Autodesk Revit"` in Global/Latest), then `28 0f 04 00 00 00 e2 00 00 00
  6c 0f 00 00 f3 83 00 00 65 00 00 00 00 00 00 00`, then the next `1f 8b 08`
  member. First marker at raw offset 6,321,402 (0x6074fa).
- Raw-inflating (`zlib` wbits=-15) starting 132 bytes after that marker's end
  yields a further **37,395-byte block** (`b4 c2 10 00 …`, same signature as
  carved members) that the current carver missed → deflate segments exist in
  the gaps that are not preceded by a `1f 8b` header (or the carver's member
  boundary detection stops early).
- Largest gaps: 93,127 bytes at raw 3,197,063 (immediately after member 095 =
  the ProjectInformation block), 54,509 at 4,301,144, 46,203 at 318,602.
  Element data (and strings) are being missed there.

Suggested: re-carve Partitions on the *block framing* rather than on gzip
magic; treat `1f 8b` as one sub-record type inside a block.

## 2. Global/Latest unit-schema JSON is scrambled/corrupt (A5 owner)

`Global/Latest` embeds ~1,500 ForgeTypeId schema documents
(`{ "annotation": …, "typeid": "autodesk.unit.unit:…" }`) as UTF-16 AStrings
(e.g. racbasic `+0x221bb`: `26 00 00 00 "autodesk.unit.dimension:currency-1.0.0"
| ca 00 00 00 "{\n   \"annotation\": …"`). But large regions are garbled:
racbasic shows dropped/inserted bytes mid-string (`61 00 75 00 00 65 6f 00 64
00 2c 00 73 00 6b` for `"autodesk"`), and rstbasic's Global/Latest is far worse
(top strings `ust ofd` ×1795, `.oudnoiotf.`). Either (a) a genuine
front-coded / interleaved string encoding in `ADocument`, or (b) our
Global/Latest inflate is wrong (racbasic member consumed 116,552 of 117,192
compressed bytes; there are 630 trailing bytes; possibly multiple deflate
segments concatenated with framing). Worth checking whether the inflated
Latest is byte-exact for other agents' decoders.

## 4. Well-known workset GUID (A3/A6 owners)

byte-identically in racbasic, rstbasic, racadvanced, rmebasic: `Contents+0x46`

GUID — a stable anchor for locating workset/partition references.
