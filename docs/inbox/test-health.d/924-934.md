## #924 — root cause of each red, and what changed

Every failure below reproduced in a fresh-clone-shaped worktree (no `samples/`, no built probes).
Repo history before `6f33fb7` (2026-08-11) is squashed, so "since when" is bisected inside the
82 commits that exist, by building the same product at each commit from `git archive` trees.

1. **`tests/test_catchain.py::test_materials_machinery`** (`assert 30 == 18`). Stale pin. The
   catprobe materials recipe adds exactly ONE value row (the -1005500 material row). `18` was the
   panelboard's 17 rows + 1 at the time the pin was written; the product has since gained rows on
   purpose (24 at `b9bdfb4^` — before any history we have — 29 today: Width/Depth/Height drives
   #931, NEC clearance toggles #887/#818/#820, standard parameters #601). The recipe never pads
   to the donor's count. Re-pinned to the law: `len(fp) == len(base rows) + 1` AND the other rows
   are the base product's rows unchanged (stronger than a count).
2. **`tests/test_catchain.py::test_full_conjunction_flips_and_roundtrips`** (group order). Stale
   pin, not the #333 re-ranking the issue guessed: the base product now authors `visibility`
   (clearance toggles, from #887 `b9bdfb4`), `constraints` and `electricalLoads` groups. The
   recipe's law is unchanged and is now pinned exactly: `keys == ["materials"] + <the base
   product's own group order>`, plus the donor's four groups still appear in donor order, no dup
   keys, roundtrip clean.
3. **`tests/test_selfcontained.py::TestRebindLane::test_apply_render_binds_makes_walk_self_contained`**
   (`assert 9 == 1`). Stale pin. `apply_render_binds` binds per `ExtrusionElem`: 1 Geometry node,
   its 6 faces, its 4 sketch curves, its material field. PP-1 had ONE box extrusion until #887
   (`b9bdfb4`: 3 — body + clearance zones), and 9 since #931/#933 (front parts). Measured at each
   commit: `b9bdfb4^` 1/6/4/1, `b9bdfb4` 3/18/12/3, `HEAD` 9/54/36/9. Re-pinned per extrusion:
   `solid == n_ext`, `faces == 6 n_ext`, `curves == 4 n_ext`, `material == n_ext`, `n_ext >= 1`
   (every extrusion bound, none skipped — strictly what the old pin checked for n = 1).
4. **`tests/test_required_settings.py::test_refplane_cutvec_is_the_plane_normal`**. Stale pin,
   broken by `9cdb82d` (#931): 6 RefPlanes through `19f2474`, 26 at `9cdb82d` (the panelboard's
   Height now goes through `height_law`, its Width/Depth through `drive_law`). I could not
   reproduce the issue comment's "fails at `b345288`" in a sample-less tree (6 planes, all
   [0,0,1], through `19f2474`). The law the test guards — `m_cutVec` is the direction of the view
   the plane was sketched in, never the old in-plane vector — holds on every plane; what changed
   is that not every plane is plan-drawn any more: `height_law` adds the template's origin
   elevation plane in its born drawn form (front elevation, cut (0,1,0)) and born surface-only
   horizontal planes (ends, cut all zero, `genDbViewId` -1). Re-pinned per generating view:
   plan-drawn → [0,0,1] (both centre planes present, ≥ 6), elevation-drawn → (0,1,0) (exactly
   one), surface-only → all zero (≥ 1), and every drawn plane's cut is orthogonal to its drawn
   line (the old defect's shape).

## #934 — the shard's wall time

Profile (`--durations`, this container, 4 cores, slow): the shard was dominated by one file,
`tests/test_archetype_alias_order_812.py` — ~537 s of ~1670 s (six sweep tests over 310,980
`resolve_prompt` calls; `_chains` alone is 106,696 prompts, not the 16,344 its docstring says).
Every other file is ≤ ~90 s and already shares its expensive builds through module fixtures
(`test_frontdoor`'s circuit jobs, `test_family_anatomy_837`'s families, ...); the long tail is
~5,000 tests of 0.1–3 s each.

Change: `resolve_prompt` itself got cheaper (`src/rvt/famgen/archetypes.py`), no test touched:
- the per-parameter alias patterns are compiled once and cached (`_alias_rx`, `_candidates`,
  `_alias_re_c`), keyed on the identity of the frozen `Param` / parameter tuple (`_by_identity`;
  `lru_cache` hashed every field of every `Param` per lookup) — same pattern strings, same order;
- a pattern whose alias does not occur in the prompt is skipped (`_alias_rx_tagged`,
  `_present_aliases`): both phrasings of an alias contain the alias's own regex as a required
  part, so such a pattern can never match — skipping it changes no result;
- `_to_number`, `_unit_of` and the brand hints use precompiled regexes (same regex text).

Why coverage is unchanged: no test, no prompt, no assertion changed — every sweep still resolves
every prompt it did and checks the same oracle. Equivalence of the engine change was measured,
not assumed: a differential hashing `(arch, values, provenance, quoted, derived, out_of_range,
name, claim, clearance)` of `resolve_prompt` over 447,654 prompts (TABLE, every `#812` generator
with and without `product=`, `_crossed` + `_chains` through product detection, and 15,776 string
literals harvested from `tests/*.py`) gives `fe1b2043…94b1e1` on both `3bb380f` and this head.
